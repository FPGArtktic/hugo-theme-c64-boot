The shortest program that proves a machine is alive.

{{< basic >}}
10 REM THE CUSTOMARY GREETING
20 PRINT "HELLO WORLD"
30 GOTO 20
{{< /basic >}}

`RUN` it and the screen fills. `RUN STOP` and it stops. That is the whole
contract, and it has never needed a changelog.
