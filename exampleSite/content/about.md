## Who

Mateusz Okulanis. Hardware-adjacent software: FPGAs, embedded Linux, and the
odd detour into the machines that started it.

## Why a C64 screen

The first computer I used printed `READY.` and then waited. It did not
suggest anything, it did not phone home, and it did not load a megabyte of
JavaScript to show forty columns of text. This site is a small argument that
the pattern still works.
